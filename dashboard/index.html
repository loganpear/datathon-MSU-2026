How the numbers are calculated (case addendum §1)

  • Ads: sum of predicted_ad_revenue_per_month over the users shown ads. Data: data_value_score/100 × $0.90. Subscription: price × subscription_adoption_probability for users whose max_subscription_price ≥ price. Brand: brand_insight_value_score/100 × $0.55 × the ramp (1.3% in month 1, 18% in month 8, 100% from month 12).
  • Churn per user: ad churn if shown ads; data-sale churn for everyone if data is licensed; ad + data (capped at 1) if both; otherwise baseline. Churn is spread over months 1–3: revenue × (1 − churn × m/3), then × (1 − churn) from month 3 on.
  • Users lost = Σ churn × 160. Extra users lost = Σ (churn − baseline) × 160, compared with doing nothing. Avg trust loss = (Σ trust_loss_if_ads over ad users + Σ trust_loss_if_data_sold over all users if licensing) ÷ 20,000.
  • Runway: cash after each month = previous − burn + revenue. Counts the months before cash drops below $0, over a 120-month horizon.
  • Raw data, unchanged. Blank cells stay blank and outliers stay in. When a value needed for a calculation is blank, that user just gets nothing from that piece: no ad revenue (and no ads), no subscription eligibility, or no trust-loss contribution. The other fields are used as they are.
  • “Find best settings” tries prices from $0.99 to $11.99 in $0.50 steps, privacy caps from 10 to 100 in steps of 10, and ad-churn caps from 0.10 to 0.85 in steps of 0.05. Among settings that reach the runway target it keeps the one with the fewest extra users lost. If none reach it, it keeps the longest runway.
